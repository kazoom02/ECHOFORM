# ECHOFORM — Intro Lore Video · Gemini Omni Flash Prompts

Tuned for **Gemini Omni Flash** in Flow. 8 clips (up to 10s each, 720p) you generate with your reference images, then stitch with your text cards + music. Omni is conversational and reference-driven, so each prompt is written as a direct instruction that points at your input image.

---

## How Omni Flash works (read first)

**Reference-driven:** For every shot you drop in the reference image(s) and the prompt tells Omni how to animate them. Your three anchors:
- **Vestige** → your character image (shots 4, 5, 6, 7, 8)
- **Slimes** → your slime image(s) (shots 3, 7)
- **Loom** → your Loom lab image (shots 1, 2, 8)

**Point-and-fix, don't reroll.** Omni's whole advantage is conversational editing. When a clip is 80% there, *tell it what to fix* instead of regenerating:
- Too fast → *"Slow the whole thing down, make the motion take the full clip."*
- Face/design warps → *"Keep his face, armor and colors stable and consistent the whole time."*
- Too busy → *"Fewer particles, calmer, cleaner motion."*
- Wrong camera → *"Less camera movement, hold the shot steadier."*

**Specs:** 720p, up to 10s, native audio. Add the lore **text cards in your editor** (ShareTechMono / ChakraPetch fonts from your project) — don't rely on Omni to render text. Keep or delete each *Audio:* line as you like.

## SHOT 1 — The Loom awakens · reference: Loom image

**Prompt:**
> Using this image of the Loom machine as the scene, animate a slow, reverent camera push-in toward the glowing cyan core. Make the concentric rings rotate slowly, the threads at the center shimmer and pulse with light, and dust motes drift through the hazy air. Keep the dark lab and all its equipment exactly as they are in the image. Cinematic, ominous, heavy cyan bloom, very slow motion.
> *Audio:* a low industrial hum, a distant mechanical heartbeat.

**Text card:** *"The world was kept alive by the Loom — a machine built to copy living things, so nothing would ever truly die."*

## SHOT 2 — It forgets how to stop · reference: Loom image

**Prompt:**
> Using this Loom image, animate the machine going into overload. The concentric rings spin faster and faster, the cyan core flares brighter and brighter and its light washes across the dark lab, and a glitch ripple stutters and briefly doubles the whole image. Faint red warning lights flicker on the monitors around the room. Tense, unstable, harsh bloom.
> *Audio:* a rising electrical whine, a sharp digital glitch stutter.

**Text card:** *"It worked. Until it forgot how to stop."*

## SHOT 3 — The ocean of slimes · reference: slime image

**Prompt:**
> Using this coral slime as reference, animate a vast dark flooded lab chamber overrun by an endless tide of these glowing slimes. Show them splitting and multiplying — one becomes two, two become four — while the camera slowly pulls back to reveal the overwhelming swarm filling the room. Near-black surroundings lit only by the sickly coral glow. Claustrophobic and dreadful.
> *Audio:* wet multiplying squelches, an uneasy swelling drone.

**Text card:** *"The first things it touched were slimes — simple cells, never meant to think, now dividing forever."*

## SHOT 4 — Vestige is printed · reference: Vestige image

**Prompt:**
> Using this character as reference, animate him being printed into existence inside a vertical shaft of cyan light — his body forming line by line out of the light, with a faint offset duplicate silhouette lingering behind him. His glowing cyan katana ignites, and he slowly lifts his head and opens his eyes. Dark machine interior, intense bloom on the blade, solemn and haunting, slow motion.
> *Audio:* a soft rising synth chord, the ring of a blade powering on.

**Text card:** *"You are Vestige — the ninth copy of a hero who fell long ago."*

## SHOT 5 — Death and rebirth · reference: Vestige image

**Prompt:**
> Using this character as the reference, animate a slow, mournful shot. The swordsman sinks to his knees and his body breaks apart into drifting cyan particles and glitch fragments until he has completely dissolved. Then, in the exact same spot, a beam of cyan light appears and an identical version of him slowly reforms from the particles and rises to stand again. Keep one faint, glitchy after-image of him lingering behind for a moment before it fades. Dark laboratory, near-black background, flickering cyan bloom, very slow motion, minimal camera movement.
> *Audio:* a slow melancholy pulse, faint digital echoes.

**Text card:** *"Each time you die, the Loom prints another you — a little more degraded than the last."*

He raises one hand, then closes it into a fist and his cyan blade flares brighter. Dark lab, soft particle glow, heavy bloom, resolute. Slow, steady motion.

## SHOT 7 — The cruel joke · references: Vestige + slime images

**Prompt:**
> Using this swordsman and this coral slime as references, animate the swordsman slashing his glowing cyan blade through a single slime in one bright arc — and on impact the slime splits into two glowing slimes that quiver as if about to split again. He stands amid a growing swarm, blade raised, outnumbered. Dark side-on arena lit by cyan and coral glow, dramatic bloom, rising dread.
> *Audio:* a blade slash, a wet split, an uneasy swell of many voices.

**Text card:** *"To end the copying you must reach the Loom's core and cut the thread. But to kill a slime is to make two more."*

## SHOT 8 — Title reveal · references: Vestige + Loom images

**Prompt:**
> Using this Loom scene and this character, animate the swordsman standing small on a foreground catwalk far below the towering glowing Loom, his cyan blade lit. Everything slowly dims except that single point of blade-light, then a hard cut to black. Cold dim lab, extreme bloom on the last cyan glow, epic and final, very slow motion.
> *Audio:* a final deep resonant tone, then silence.

**Text card (final):** *"Every death is a birth."* → hard-cut to your **ECHOFORM** logo (a dividing cell forming the "O").

## Stitching notes
- Keep the cyan `#00FFFF` blade, near-black backgrounds, and the painterly 2D look **consistent** across all 8 clips — it's the visual thread of the game.
- 845 credits is plenty — draft freely, and use point-and-fix chat to refine rather than burning credits on full rerolls.
- Assemble text cards, logo, music and SFX in an editor (CapCut is free and easy). Mono/tech font (ShareTechMono or ChakraPetch) matches your Neural Interface HUD.
