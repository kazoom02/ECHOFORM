### If the single-clip morph looks unstable
Omni handles one background plate per clip best. For the cleanest result, split into two 5s clips and cut between them:
1. **Depart Area 1** — *"Using the purple market image and this character, Vestige walks left to right and exits frame right, rain falling, cyan blade lighting the puddles. Hold the environment exactly as in the image."*
2. **Arrive Area 2** — *"Using the blue city image and this character, Vestige walks in from frame left onto the wet plaza and stops, looking up at the towers. Keep the blue skyline exactly as in the image."*

### Point-and-fix tips
- Walk too fast → *"Slow his walk, make the stride cover the full clip."*
- Design warps → *"Keep his face, armor and cyan colors stable and consistent throughout."*
- Backgrounds blend messily → *"Keep the two environments distinct; transition the palette gradually, don't melt the buildings."*
- Rain too heavy → *"Lighter rain, keep the neon reflections readable."*
