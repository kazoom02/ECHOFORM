### If the single-clip morph looks unstable
Omni handles one background plate per clip best. Split into two 5s clips and cut on the threshold:
1. **Leave Area 2** — *"Using the blue rainy city image and this character, Vestige walks left to right across the wet plaza and exits frame right toward a dark doorway, rain falling, cyan blade lighting the puddles. Hold the city exactly as in the image."*
2. **Enter Area 3** — *"Using the Loom laboratory image and this character, Vestige walks in from frame left onto the catwalk and stops, looking up at the glowing Loom core, no rain, deep cyan bloom. Keep the chamber exactly as in the image."*

### Point-and-fix tips
- Walk too fast → *"Slow his walk, make the stride cover the full clip."*
- Design warps → *"Keep his face, armor and cyan colors stable and consistent throughout."*
- Core too dim → *"Make the Loom core brighter and pulsing, strongest light in the shot."*
- Transition melts → *"Keep the two environments distinct; cut the rain the moment he crosses the doorway."*
